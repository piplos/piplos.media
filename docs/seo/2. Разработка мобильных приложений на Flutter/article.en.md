## Why Flutter is the right choice for business

**Budget efficiency.** A single codebase for iOS and Android cuts development hours and support cost. One team instead of two native squads — less coordination, unified releases, a predictable roadmap.

**Faster time to market.** Hot Reload speeds up iterations: UI changes appear in seconds, not after a full rebuild. You reach users sooner, validate hypotheses faster and collect feedback earlier.

**UI without compromise.** Flutter renders through its own engine — the app looks and runs smoothly across screen sizes. Complex animations, custom components and branded design — no "great on one platform, stripped down on the other". Alibaba, BMW and Google rely on Flutter — it is a mature choice for product teams, not a cheap shortcut.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Flutter suitable for complex apps?

Yes. Flutter powers fintech, e-commerce, streaming and enterprise systems. Limits appear only with deep platform-specific needs — then we combine Flutter with native modules.

### How long does an MVP take?

A typical MVP runs 6–10 weeks depending on features, integrations and design readiness. Hot Reload and a single codebase accelerate iterations compared to two native codebases.

### Who maintains the app after launch?

We handle technical support: iOS and Android OS updates, bug fixes, feature development. We also deliver documentation and clean code if you prefer an in-house team to take over.
